# Complete Marketing Solution Specification
## Modeled After HubSpot (2025)

---

## Executive Summary

This specification outlines a comprehensive marketing platform modeled after industry-leading solutions like HubSpot. The platform provides an all-in-one customer platform combining CRM, marketing automation, content management, sales enablement, and customer service capabilities with AI-powered features throughout.

---

## 1. Platform Architecture

### 1.1 Core Platform Structure

The platform follows a modular "Hub" architecture where customers can adopt individual hubs or combine them into integrated solutions:

- **Marketing Hub**: Campaign management, email marketing, automation, lead generation
- **Sales Hub**: Pipeline management, deal tracking, sales automation
- **Service Hub**: Customer support, ticketing, knowledge base
- **Content Hub (CMS)**: Website builder, content management, SEO optimization
- **Data Hub**: Data integration, synchronization, governance
- **Operations Hub**: Workflow automation, data quality, programmability

### 1.2 Unified Data Layer

- **Smart CRM**: Central database storing all customer interactions, contacts, companies, deals, and activities
- **Real-time synchronization**: All hubs share data instantly through unified data layer
- **Universal search**: Search across all records, content, and activities from any hub
- **Custom objects**: Ability to create custom data structures beyond standard objects
- **Data enrichment**: Automatic enhancement with external data sources (200M+ profiles)

### 1.3 AI Foundation (Breeze AI Layer)

Three core AI components integrated throughout the platform:

1. **Breeze Assistant (Copilot)**
   - AI virtual assistant acting as personal chief of staff
   - Can create workflows, summarize CRM records, generate content
   - Context-aware understanding of business data and goals
   - Accessible throughout the platform interface

2. **Breeze Agents**
   - **Customer Agent**: 24/7 automated support responses trained on knowledge base
   - **Data Agent**: Automated customer intelligence gathering from CRM and web
   - **Social Media Agent**: Performance analysis, content creation, scheduling optimization
   - **Content Agent**: Data-informed topic suggestions, automatic internal linking, context-aware generation

3. **Breeze Intelligence**
   - Access to 200M+ buyer and company profiles
   - Automatic contact enrichment and validation
   - Duplicate detection and data cleansing
   - AI-powered field completion

### 9.1.1 Advanced Ticket Search

**Search Scope & Context:**
- Business area filtering (search within specific departments, teams, or business units)
- Cross-functional search across all accessible tickets
- Saved search templates for common queries
- Recent searches history
- Search result export capabilities

**Text-Based Search:**
- **Subject/Title Search**: Fast search across ticket subjects with autocomplete suggestions
- **Body/Description Search**: Full-text search within ticket descriptions
- **Deep Search in Updates**: Search through all ticket comments, internal notes, and update history
- **Attachment Content Search**: Search within attached documents (PDF, Word, Excel, etc.)
- **Keyword highlighting**: Visual highlighting of search terms in results
- **Boolean operators**: Support for AND, OR, NOT operators
- **Phrase search**: Exact phrase matching with quotation marks
- **Wildcard search**: Pattern matching with * and ? wildcards

**User-Based Filters:**
- **Logger/Reporter**: Filter by ticket creator/submitter
  - Single or multiple users
  - User groups or teams
  - Department-based filtering
- **Assignee**: Filter by current assignee
  - Individual assignees
  - Team assignments
  - Unassigned tickets
  - Reassignment history
- **Watchers/Followers**: Filter by users watching the ticket
- **Last Updated By**: Filter by who made the most recent update

**Status & State Filters:**
- **Ticket State**:
  - Open, In Progress, Pending, On Hold, Resolved, Closed
  - Custom state definitions
  - Multiple state selection
  - State transition history
- **Resolution Status**: Resolved vs. Unresolved
- **Closure Status**: Open vs. Closed
- **Reopened Tickets**: Identify tickets that were reopened after closure

**Category & Classification:**
- **Primary Category**: Main ticket categorization (Bug, Feature Request, Support, etc.)
- **Sub-category**: Hierarchical categorization up to 3 levels
- **Tags**: Multiple tag-based filtering with AND/OR logic
- **Product/Module**: Filter by affected product, module, or component
- **Severity/Impact**: Critical, High, Medium, Low
- **Customer Type**: Enterprise, Professional, Starter, Free tier

**Date-Based Filters:**

*Created/Logged Date:*
- Specific date
- Date range (from/to)
- Quick filters:
  - Today
  - Yesterday
  - Past 1 week
  - Past 2 weeks
  - Past 1 month
  - Past 3 months
  - Past 6 months
  - Past year
  - Custom date range
- Relative date filters (e.g., "older than 30 days")

*Last Updated Date:*
- Same quick filter options as Created Date
- Updated within specific timeframe
- Not updated since (identify stale tickets)
- Recent activity (updated in last X hours/days)

*Closed Date:*
- Closure date range
- Quick filters for recently closed tickets
- Tickets closed within SLA vs. outside SLA

*Due Date:*
- Overdue tickets
- Due today/this week/this month
- Upcoming deadlines

*First Response Time:*
- Responded within X hours/days
- Missed first response SLA
- No response yet

*Resolution Time:*
- Resolved within X hours/days
- Resolved within SLA vs. outside SLA

**Metadata Field Filters:**
- **Priority**: Urgent, High, Medium, Low
- **Source**: Email, Chat, Phone, Portal, API
- **Company/Account**: Filter by customer company
- **Contact**: Filter by associated contact
- **Deal/Opportunity**: Tickets linked to specific deals
- **Product Version**: Affected product version
- **Environment**: Production, Staging, Development
- **Region/Geography**: Customer location-based filtering
- **Language**: Ticket language
- **Custom Fields**: Any custom metadata fields defined

**SLA & Performance Metrics:**
- Within SLA vs. Breached SLA
- Time to first response
- Time to resolution
- Time in current status
- Number of reassignments
- Number of reopens
- Customer satisfaction score

- Combine multiple filter criteria with AND/OR logic

- Filter exclusions (NOT operator)

*Saved Searches:*
- Save frequently used search queries
- Name and organize saved searches

*Search Performance:*
